Global-mean Δ¹⁴C

The kriged field tracks Graven's independent CMIP6 compilation to within about 1‰ across the whole period. Hua sits consistently above everything.

hua_shape is identical to hua_geom here by construction — it is Hua's contrast re-levelled onto our own global mean, so only one line is drawn. The two separate in the isoflux chart below.

Past 2019 hua_pub is the paper's own exponential, not data — and it decays faster than the stations do, crossing from ~7‰ above the others to below them by 2023. Our network runs to 2025, so for the later years Hua is extrapolated where we are observed.

Interhemispheric contrast, NH − SH

Compilations, not the variants above. Our stations and Hua agree that the northern deficit is real and growing; Graven's is roughly half the size. After 2019 Hua's NH and SH arms extrapolate on different decay constants, so the contrast between them widens mechanically rather than observationally — the divergence right of the marker is an artefact, not a measurement.

Global ocean disequilibrium isoflux

The ocean's ¹⁴C isoflux is heading through zero — and which year it crosses depends on the Δ_atm choice.

By 2023 the candidates disagree about the sign. With the kriged field the global ocean isoflux is already +207 PgC‰/yr — the ocean has become a net ¹⁴C source. With the band field currently in use it is still −91. That is not a rounding difference; it is a qualitative claim about the contemporary carbon cycle resting on a forcing choice nobody had tested.

Reading it

Three findings survive the comparison. First, a two-parameter step at the Equator is as good as the full kriged field — 2.918 against 2.918. Hua's geometry is vindicated, and it independently confirms the −6‰ interhemispheric contrast our own stations measure.

Second, Hua's disagreement is level, not shape. That is what makes it usable: the contrast can be taken from Hua and the absolute value from somewhere better constrained. In practice this barely matters — once the level is fixed, our gradient and Hua's differ by about 1% of the isoflux.

Third, the +10‰ tropical band is rejected by this network. It scores worse than adding no tropical structure at all, and fitting the hemispheric values from extratropical stations only — which is how Miller's construction actually works — does not rescue it (3.867).

The one open judgement call. That band rests on UC Irvine Pacific shipboard transects which are not in this network, and our coverage between 10°N and 40°S is three sites. Our stations positively disagree with it, which is evidence — not proof. Dropping it is a decision, not a derivation.

On the numbers here the recommendation is to keep the kriged field, point ocedis at it instead of the band field, and treat Hua as the independent corroboration it turns out to be. The larger prize is still unclaimed: giving atmos_del a latitude index so the ~6‰ gradient reaches the transported tracer at all.
